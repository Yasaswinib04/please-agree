**One-liner:** Yomnita is a classroom intelligence tool that turns exam evaluations into a real understanding of each student's strengths and weaknesses — going deeper than the score, while giving teachers their time back.

**How it works:** Teachers photograph answer sheets on a phone (no scanner needed) and upload them with the answer key → Yomnita grades MCQs and written answers → the teacher reviews and can override every mark in a clean UI → the school gets per-student, per-question insight into where each child is actually struggling.

**The wedge vs. the vision:** Grading is the entry point — it's the painful, time-consuming job nobody wants. The value is what grading unlocks: an objective, per-question picture of student understanding that a marks register can never give you.

**Who it's for:** Private K-12 schools. Works with CBSE and State syllabus (AP/Telangana board) exams. English-medium answer sheets today; regional-medium on the roadmap.

**Differentiator vs. big edtech suites:** Most tools (LMS/ERP suites) manage schools; almost none actually grade handwritten answer sheets — and nearly all target CBSE. Yomnita is grading-first, insight-led, and supports State syllabus schools that everyone else ignores.

## What to pitch a principal — and what NOT to

**Do NOT lead with parent retention/churn.** Verified with a school founder: parent churn at established private schools is already low, so "reduce churn" is a weak, unbelievable lever and it signals we don't know the sector.

**Lead with these instead:**
1. **Teacher time and burnout** — the ~10 hours/week, and what those hours could become.
2. **Beyond marks-based evaluation** — a marks register says a child scored 62. It never says *which concepts* they lost marks on. Even a school whose whole focus is board results needs to understand the student to improve the result.
3. **Differentiation of the learning system** — being the school that can show parents per-concept progress, not just a rank.

## Objections to expect (answer honestly)

- *"Photographing every sheet is extra work."* → It's phone photos, not scanning — the Class 9 pilot ran entirely on WhatsApp-quality images. Net time is still strongly positive; quantify with the Tier 2 numbers.
- *"How accurate is it on our children's handwriting?"* → Be straight: handwriting recognition is the hardest part and the teacher reviews every mark. Nothing is released without teacher sign-off. Show MCQ agreement (Tier 2) as the concrete number.
- *"We already have an ERP/LMS."* → Those manage the school; they don't grade answer sheets. Yomnita sits alongside, not instead.
