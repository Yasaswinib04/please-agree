**One-liner:**
Cuemath provides 1:1 personalized online math tutoring for students K-8, delivered live by certified tutors through an interactive gamified platform, designed to build both math proficiency and problem-solving confidence ("MathFit™").

**How it works:**
- Parents fill out a form describing their child's needs and personality
- A counsellor matches the child with a tutor and arranges a free trial session
- If satisfied, parents choose a plan (2-3 classes/week, 6- or 12-month commitment)
- Students attend live 40-minute 1:1 sessions via an interactive online platform
- Each child receives a personalized learning path based on strengths and gaps
- Curriculum is school-aligned and covers homework help, exam prep, mental math, remedial support, and advanced learning for competitions (e.g., IMO)
- Parents track progress via a dedicated app

**Who it's for:**
- Parents of K-8 students seeking personalized math support beyond classroom instruction

- High-performing students preparing for competitive math exams (IMO, Gauss Contest, etc.)
- Families in 80+ countries (strong presence in India, also US, international markets)
- Schools exploring supplemental tutoring partnerships or after-school enrichment programs

**Differentiator:**
- **1:1 live instruction** (not pre-recorded, not group classes) with top 1% certified tutors
- **MathFit™ philosophy**: builds both math proficiency and confident problem-solving mindset together
- **Personalized learning paths** adapted to each student's pace and needs
- **School-aligned curriculum** + competitive exam prep in one program
- **Flexible scheduling** with leave options and easy rescheduling via parent app
- Founded 2013, Google-backed, 200K+ students, 4.9/5 rating across 11K+ reviews

## What to pitch — and what NOT to

### ✅ DO pitch:
- **Supplemental support that complements your curriculum** — we align to school standards and help with homework, not replace classroom teaching
- **Personalized intervention at scale** — every child gets 1:1 attention, which most schools can't resource internally
- **Flexible partnership models** — after-school enrichment, remedial support cohorts, competition prep for advanced students
- **Parent-funded or school-subsidized options** — we can work with schools on group pricing or referral partnerships
- **Proven international scale** (200K students, 80 countries) with strong parent satisfaction (4.9/5)
- **Free trial for school families** — let parents and students experience it before committing

### ❌ DO NOT pitch:
- "We'll replace your math teachers" — you won't, and schools will shut down immediately
- "Guaranteed test score gains" — you have testimonials, not controlled evidence (yet)
- "Every child will love math" — aspirational, not measurable or guaranteed
- "Cheaper than hiring staff" — even if true, it sounds like you're undermining teachers' value
- Hard ROI claims without school-specific data — move those to Tier 3 hypotheses

## Objections to expect

**"We already have after-school tutoring / intervention programs."**
- *Response:* "That's great — how many students can you serve 1:1 each week? Most schools tell us their programs are group-based or under-resourced. Cuemath can extend your reach, especially for students who need individualized pacing or have math anxiety that makes group settings hard."

**"Parents won't pay for this."**
- *Response:* "We work with schools in two ways: parent-funded (we offer a free trial and flexible plans) or school-subsidized for target cohorts like Title I students or remedial groups. We can also do a pilot with a small group to show demand before scaling."

**"Our teachers won't support an outside tutoring program."**
- *Response:* "We position Cuemath as a partner, not a replacement. Teachers get a parent app report showing what each child is working on, so they can see the support aligning with classroom goals. Many teachers appreciate the homework help and confidence-building that makes their classroom time more productive."

**"How do we know it works?"**
- *Response:* "Fair question. We have 200K students and a 4.9 rating, but those are parent reviews, not school data. That's why we propose a pilot: we'll measure baseline scores, track progress, and survey teachers and parents after 12 weeks. You'll have your own data to decide."

**"Screen time concerns — parents/teachers worry about more online learning."**
- *Response:* "Understandable. Cuemath is live and interactive, not passive video watching. The 40-minute sessions are highly engaging, and the tutor adapts in real time. Many parents tell us kids actually look forward to the sessions because they feel supported, not stressed."

**"What if the tutor doesn't match the child?"**
- *Response:* "We match based on personality and learning needs, and if it's not working, we re-match. The free trial lets families experience the fit before committing. Flexibility is built in."

**"We need curriculum control / alignment to our standards."**
- *Response:* "Our curriculum is school-aligned by design — we cover the same topics your teachers do, just personalized to each child's pace. We're happy to share scope and sequence documents and coordinate with your math lead to ensure alignment."
